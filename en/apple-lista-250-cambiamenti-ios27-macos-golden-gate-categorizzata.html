Apple releases official categorized list of 250+ changes in iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate

MacRumors has catalogued the full slide briefly shown at the keynote: over 250 changes across iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate, watchOS 27, tvOS 27, and visionOS 27, now organized by category.

Published: 2026-06-11T01:26:06.232378+02:00 Topic area: Operating systems

The 250+ slide, finally readable

During the WWDC 2026 keynote, Apple briefly displayed a dense slide of minor features across all new OS generations. MacRumors has now published a full categorized list of over 250 entries, making accessible material that was practically unreadable during the live event.

Notable iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 entries include: using two iPhones with the same phone number, smoother Wi-Fi to cellular transitions, and new keyboards for Indigenous North American languages including Blackfoot, Comanche, Cree, Kiowa, and Tsuu'tina, alongside already-noted additions like Afrikaans, Baybayin, and Guaraní.

A working document for developers and users

The categorized list published on June 10 by MacRumors is already a reference for developers and journalists wanting to dig into individual features. All operating systems are available as developer betas since June 8, with public release expected in September.
