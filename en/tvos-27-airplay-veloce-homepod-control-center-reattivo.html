tvOS 27: faster AirPlay to HomePod and redesigned Control Center

tvOS 27 brings faster AirPlay connections to HomePod, a more responsive Control Center, and a redesigned Podcasts app: minor keynote announcements but relevant for those using Apple TV as a daily audio-video hub.

Published: 2026-06-10T01:03:07.099448+02:00 Topic area: Services and ecosystem

tvOS: the quiet platform

Apple dedicated little time to tvOS 27 in the keynote, as has been the case for years. Yet the concrete additions are three and target exactly those who use Apple TV as the center of their daily home audio-video setup. Thurrott and other post-keynote coverage sites collected them in their analysis of minor announcements.

The three operational additions

First: AirPlay connections to HomePod become faster. Not a spectacular update, but anyone who has experienced the lag between selecting the speaker and actual audio starting will know it is a quality-of-life improvement. Second: the Control Center in tvOS 27 is more responsive, with reduced latency making access to audio controls, picture-in-picture mode, and settings less frustrating. Third: the Podcasts app receives a redesign, likely aligned to the Liquid Glass aesthetic of the other OSes, with new navigation features.

Compatibility: a clean cut

As previously reported, tvOS 27 is not available on Apple TV HD (2015) and the first-generation Apple TV 4K (2017). Those who still have one of these devices will not receive any future updates. The message is clear: it is time to move to the current generation if you want to keep up with software updates.
