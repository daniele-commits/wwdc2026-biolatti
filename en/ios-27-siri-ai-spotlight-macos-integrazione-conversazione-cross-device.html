Siri AI integrates into macOS Spotlight and continues conversations across devices

On macOS Golden Gate, Siri AI is integrated directly into Spotlight. Conversations can then be resumed on another device — iPhone or Mac — without losing context.

Published: 2026-06-10T02:03:12.333334+02:00 Topic area: Apple Intelligence and Siri

Spotlight as the AI portal on Mac

With macOS Golden Gate, Apple brings Siri AI directly inside Spotlight: no separate window opens; the assistant is accessible from the same search bar Mac users have relied on for years. According to Yahoo Tech, most Siri integrations available on iOS will also be available on macOS, making the Mac a first-class client for Apple's AI — not just an iPhone appendage.

Conversation continuity

Perhaps the most underappreciated feature is cross-device conversation continuity. Siri AI is accompanied by a dedicated app available across all Apple platforms: starting a conversation on iPhone and resuming it on Mac becomes possible without reformulating context. Yahoo Tech reports this as a direct consequence of the new architecture, which keeps chat history synchronized across devices. It's a step toward truly personal AI — but its usefulness depends entirely on how reliable Siri AI proves to be in everyday practice.
