Apple Newsroom: official press release on iOS 27, iPadOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate

Apple published on its Newsroom the official press release describing the next generation of Apple Intelligence, Siri AI, and all operating system updates announced at WWDC 2026.

Published: 2026-06-11T02:03:11.236154+02:00 Topic area: Operating systems

Apple's voice, unfiltered

Beyond media coverage, Apple published directly on its Newsroom the official WWDC 2026 press release, which serves as the primary source for technical specifications and precise feature descriptions. The document describes the next generation of Apple Intelligence and the introduction of Siri AI as the backbone of 2026 software updates.

The release is particularly useful for clarifying the nuances of Siri AI geographic limitations — including the distinction between what's available in Europe on macOS and visionOS versus the block on iOS and iPadOS — and for confirming official hardware requirements, which include iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 and later, iPad with M1 or later, Mac with M1 or later, and Apple Vision Pro.

For those developing for Apple's platform, the official press release remains the reference point for citing features in release notes or marketing communications, as media formulations all derive from this original document.
