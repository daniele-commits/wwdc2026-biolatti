Apple Design Awards 2026: 36 Finalists Across Six Categories

Apple has revealed the 36 finalists for the 2026 Apple Design Awards across six categories. Winners will be announced in the coming weeks.

Published: 2026-06-08T15:29:31.667231+02:00 Topic area: Developers

The Six Competing Categories

The Apple Design Awards annually recognize apps that demonstrate the highest level of design craft, creativity, and technical expertise. The 36 finalists for the 2026 edition are distributed across the categories Delight and Fun, Inclusivity, Innovation, Interaction, Social Impact, and Visuals and Graphics. Apple published the finalists' names on the Apple Developer website to coincide with the opening of WWDC26.

Why It Matters

The ADA is not merely a prestige recognition: being a finalist — and especially a winner — has a concrete impact on App Store visibility, often translating into editorial featuring and meaningful download increases. Winners will be announced in the weeks following the conference, as confirmed by Apple Newsroom.
